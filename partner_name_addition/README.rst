Adds the field *Name Addition* to contacts: a second name line such as a
trade name, a department or the name of a practice. It is printed in the
address of quotations, orders, invoices, delivery slips, the portal and
wherever Odoo formats an address, right below the name of the company
and above the street:

::

   Anna Beispiel
   Praxis am Markt - Physiotherapie
   Musterweg 1
   12345 Musterstadt

Odoo has no field for this. The usual workarounds put the addition into
the company name, which then shows up everywhere in one line, or into
the second street line, which Odoo prints after the street and
electronic invoices export as part of the street.

Contacts and addresses of a company use the name addition of the company
unless they have one of their own. Contacts without a name addition are
not changed.

Configuration
=============

Nothing to configure. Report layouts that print the name lines on their
own can show the name addition where they want it
(``partner.commercial_partner_id.name_addition``) and print the
remaining address with the option ``no_name_addition`` of the contact
widget, so it does not appear twice:

::

   <div t-field="o.partner_id"
        t-options="{'widget': 'contact', 'fields': ['address'], 'no_name_addition': True}"/>

In Python, the context key ``skip_name_addition`` has the same effect on
``_display_address()``.

Usage
=====

Open a contact and enter the name addition below the name. It applies at
once to every document that prints the address of this contact or of one
of its contacts and addresses.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- The name addition is not part of the display name, so it is not shown
  in lists and selection fields.
- Electronic invoices (Factur-X, XRechnung) do not contain it; they have
  no field for a second name line of the buyer.
