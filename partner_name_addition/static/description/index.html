A second name line in every address

A trade name, a department or the name of a practice, printed between the name and the street

What it does

  • A field Name Addition on the contact, right below the name.
  • Printed in every address Odoo formats: quotations, orders, invoices, delivery slips and the portal.
  • For a contact of a company, the company name keeps its own line and the name addition follows below it.
  • Odoo has no field for this. The usual workarounds put the addition into the company name, which then shows up everywhere in one line, or into the second street line, which Odoo prints after the street and electronic invoices export as part of the street.
  • Contacts and addresses of a company use the name addition of the company unless they have one of their own.
  • Contacts without a name addition are not changed.

One field on the contact

Below the name, for persons and companies

Good to know

  • Nothing to configure. Report layouts that print the name lines on their own can place the name addition themselves (partner.commercial_partner_id.name_addition) and print the rest of the address with the option no_name_addition of the contact widget.
  • The name addition is not part of the display name, so lists and selection fields do not show it.
  • Electronic invoices (Factur-X, XRechnung) do not contain it; they have no field for a second name line of the buyer.
