Adds the field *Name Addition* to contacts: a second name line such as a trade
name, a department or the name of a practice. It is printed in the address of
quotations, orders, invoices, delivery slips, the portal and wherever Odoo
formats an address, right below the name of the company and above the street:

    Anna Beispiel
    Praxis am Markt - Physiotherapie
    Musterweg 1
    12345 Musterstadt

Odoo has no field for this. The usual workarounds put the addition into the
company name, which then shows up everywhere in one line, or into the second
street line, which Odoo prints after the street and electronic invoices export
as part of the street.

Contacts and addresses of a company use the name addition of the company unless
they have one of their own. Contacts without a name addition are not changed.
